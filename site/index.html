How it works Cara kerjanya

  1. Fill in your shop and menu. Paste the menu you already send on WhatsApp, or type it in.
  2. Download your site (.zip). It is a complete menu page with its own assistant.
  3. Put it online, free. Drag the folder to a free host. Buyers and AI agents can then find you, and orders open your WhatsApp.

Privacy. Your AI key stays in your browser. The shop never sees it. Orders go straight to the shop's WhatsApp, with no account and no middleman. Kunci AI tetap di browser kamu. Pesanan langsung ke WhatsApp warung.

Put it online (free) Pasang di internet

  1. Unzip the file you downloaded. You get index.html, _headers and a folder named after your shop, <slug>.
  2. On Cloudflare (free account), open Workers & Pages, then Create, then Pages, then Upload assets.
  3. Give the project a name, drag the whole unzipped folder in (the one that holds _headers), and press Deploy.
  4. Cloudflare shows your site's address when the upload finishes (it ends in .pages.dev or .workers.dev; the first visit can take a few minutes). Your page is <address>/<slug>/, and your assistant's network address is <address>/<slug>/warung. The _headers file is already inside the zip, so other sites can read it.
  5. Join Pasar: open an issue or pull request on the project repository () with your site address, and we will list your warung.
